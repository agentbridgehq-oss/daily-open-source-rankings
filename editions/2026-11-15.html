Daily Edition — Sunday, November 15, 2026

3,400 Stars in One Day: Chrome's Web Guide Won't Let Go of #1, Android Apps Just Went Native on Windows — Today's 10 Open-Source Breakouts

Chrome's guide for coding agents added 3,400 stars in a single day — its third straight day at number one. And right behind it, a tool that lets you double-click an Android app on Windows and just run it, no emulator window in sight.

That's the shape of Sunday, November 15, 2026: agents getting better tools, and the walls between your devices getting thinner. Here's the ten.

1. GoogleChrome/modern-web-guidance (★16.6k, +3,400 today) — The skill pack stewarded by the Chrome, Edge, Firefox and MDN teams — still the biggest gainer on GitHub, and still the one to beat. Its CLI pulls curated guides to the modern web and scans your code for legacy patterns. Three days at number one, and nobody's caught it yet.

2. RoutineMiku/apkstart (★4.6k, +2,056 today) — Double-click an APK on Windows and it installs and runs in its own window — no emulator window to babysit. Its own QEMU runtime makes Android apps behave like native Windows apps. The day's second-biggest gain, and the most practical one: your phone apps, on your PC, like they belong there.

3. libracoder/kiri (★4.7k, +1,541 today) — A small, fast container runtime for macOS, Windows, and Linux in Rust — gaining 1,541 stars a day after yesterday's debut here. The pitch hasn't changed: containers without the weight, plus a free ngrok alternative baked in. Two days in, and it's already the board's most consistent climber.

4. deepseek-ai/chat-demo (★2.1k, +1,080 today) — DeepSeek's static chat demo, powered by its API — two days old and already past two thousand stars. It's the simplest proof the harness era is real: a chat front end you can run yourself, pointed at DeepSeek's models. Small repo, big signal. Prefer the same idea without anyone's API? Our free ChatGPT alternative guide runs the chat on your own machine instead.

5. backnotprop/plannotator (★11.6k, +860 today) — The number-one repo on GitHub Trending this morning. Your agents run off and do things, and you lose track — Plannotator is a local review surface: annotate plans and diffs, then send feedback to the agent in one click. An agent that shows you its plan before it acts is one you can actually trust with real work.

6. Likely7/Veyra-NRVideo (★2.6k, +731 today) — A Windows video player and capture tool that pipes video through AI super resolution, DLSS 5, and frame generation in real time — the repo's headline claim is bringing PS5 GTA 6 to 120 FPS. Take the marketing with a grain of salt: the tech is real, the frame-rate promise is the repo's, not ours.

7. tradermonty/claude-trading-skills (★5.2k, +707 today) — A Claude Skills toolkit for individual investors: market analysis, charting, economic calendars, screeners, and strategy — built around long-term ETF and dividend investing. The repo is blunt that it's not a signal service. If tickers are your thing, our top AI & tech stocks page has the same names in plain language, at Friday's close.

8. alibaba/open-code-review (★51.7k, +688 today) — Alibaba's internal AI code review tool, now open source — second straight day on this board. Deterministic pipelines plus an LLM agent write precise line-level comments, backed by a built-in multi-language ruleset. This is the review process a company of Alibaba's size actually trusts, free to run yourself.

9. ChromeDevTools/chrome-devtools-mcp (★56.6k, +664 today) — Chrome DevTools for coding agents, as an MCP server or CLI. Your agent drives a live Chrome — network analysis, screenshots, console messages with source-mapped stack traces. An agent that can see the browser is one that can actually fix the website. And if your agent needs an editor to live in, our free Cursor alternative covers the $0 setup.

10. we-promise/sure (★15.5k, +520 today) — A personal finance app you can self-host — a community fork of the abandoned Maybe Finance project, back on the board after its November 13 debut. Your money data stays on your machine. If self-hosting your money sounds like your kind of saving, our save-money page has twelve more swaps worth $1,713.52 a year at list prices.

That's your ten. My pick? apkstart — the day your phone apps run on your PC like native software, the line between the two machines gets thinner — and this one does it with a double-click. If I've got an hour free, I'm installing plannotator first: an agent that shows me its plan is one I can trust with real work.
