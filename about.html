What this site is

Daily Open Source Rankings is a daily briefing. Every morning, it ranks the 10 open-source projects most likely to blow up — picked on star velocity, novelty, and plain usefulness — and explains in plain language why each one matters.

The picks come from the day's trending data across GitHub discovery sources. The writing is original: no copied press releases, no scraped descriptions, no filler. Each edition ends with an honest pick — the one project worth your hour.
