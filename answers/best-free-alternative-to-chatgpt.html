Answer — free AI chat

What's the best free alternative to ChatGPT?

Short answer: run a local model on your own computer. Ollama downloads and runs open AI models with one command, and Open WebUI gives you a clean, ChatGPT-style chat window on top of it. Together they're free, private — your chats never leave your machine — and they work offline.

What you get: unlimited chats with no subscription and no per-message limits, a choice of open models (coding, writing, and general chat models all run this way), and full control over your history. On a modern laptop the smaller models answer fast; bigger models are slower but noticeably sharper.

Who it's for: anyone paying for a chat subscription mainly to write, summarize, brainstorm, or code — and anyone who'd rather their questions stayed on their own hard drive. If you need the absolute frontier model for hard research, a paid plan still wins on raw power. For everyday work, local chat is the free option I'd start with.

Two more from the rankings archive: Hex, a dictation app that never phones home, for talking instead of typing — and a free studio that puts 600 AI models in one place. Both made recent Top 10 lists, and new picks land every morning.
