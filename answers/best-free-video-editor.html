What's the best free video editor with no subscription?

Short answer: Kdenlive. It's a full multi-track video editor — cuts, titles, effects, color work, 4K timelines — that runs on Windows, Mac, and Linux, exports with no watermark, and costs nothing. Not a trial, not a lite version with the good parts locked: the whole editor is free and open source.

What you get: the editing depth most people actually pay a subscription for, plus a community that's maintained it for years. If you're cutting YouTube videos, shorts, or client work, it covers the job end to end.

Pair it with: OBS Studio for recording your screen or camera — also free, also open source, and the standard tool streamers already use. Record in OBS, cut in Kdenlive, and the monthly editor bill is gone.

Who it's for: creators and small businesses paying for an editor they only open a few times a month. The trade-off is honest: you'll spend an afternoon learning a new layout. After that, it's yours — no renewal notices, ever.
