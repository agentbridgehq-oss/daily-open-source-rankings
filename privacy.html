Privacy policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026.

Daily Open Source Rankings is a small editorial website run by Ken under The AI Advantage. This page explains, in plain language, what information the site collects and what happens to it.

Email subscriptions. If you use the subscribe form, the email address you enter is collected through Netlify Forms and stored so we can send you new editions of the rankings. That's the only use: one email per edition. We do not sell, rent, or share subscriber email addresses with third parties for marketing. Every email includes a way to unsubscribe, and you can also ask to be removed at any time using the contact route below — removal requests are honored promptly.

What we don't collect. Reading the site requires no account and no personal information. We do not run our own analytics trackers on this site today.

Cookies. This site itself does not set cookies. Our hosting provider (Netlify) may process basic technical logs (such as IP addresses and browser details) to deliver and secure the site. If advertising is added in the future, the ad network may set its own cookies to serve and measure ads; this policy will be updated before that happens.

Third-party links. Editions link to external projects and sites (for example GitHub repositories, our Stan store, and our newsletter). Those sites have their own privacy practices, which we don't control.

Contact. Questions, unsubscribe requests, or anything else: reach us through the subscribe form on the homepage (mention "privacy" in your message if you reply to an edition email), or via The AI Advantage newsletter.

Changes. If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
